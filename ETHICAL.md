# Ethical Statement

**Version 1.0** | Last updated: November 10, 2025

---

## Preamble

In a world organized around principles of human dignity, solidarity, and
collective care, this document would not exist. Ethical behavior would be the
default. We would not need to write down that we oppose sexual violence, that
we support gender liberation, that we reject military applications, that we
resist exploitation.

We would not need to say these things because they would already be obvious.

And yet here we are. In 2025, in a world where governments fund conversion
therapy, where affirming spaces are weaponized against trans and non-binary
people, where artificial intelligence is deployed to target marginalized
communities, where profit extraction remains the primary driver of technological
innovation, where developers often treat ethical considerations as afterthoughts
or obstacles—we find it necessary to be explicit.

This document represents our ethical commitment to the use and development of
this software. While the legal licensing of our code is governed by a specific
license (see LICENSE or LICENSE.md in this repository), we make an explicit
appeal to the conscience and values of all who use, modify, and redistribute
this software.

We believe that ethical principles precede law. They also precede technology.
The principles below should not require articulation. They should be self-evident
to anyone committed to human flourishing. Yet because they are not, because
these principles are routinely violated by corporations, governments, and
technologies claiming neutrality, we state them plainly.

The following principles are not enforced through legal mechanism, but through
ethical commitment and community accountability. We hope that those who adopt
this software will embrace not merely its technical capabilities, but the values
that guide its creation.

**If you are someone for whom these principles resonate, we welcome you. If you
choose to violate them, we cannot stop you—but we ask you to consider what kind
of world your choices help build. More importantly: we ask you to consider what
kind of person you choose to be.**

## Our Ethical Principles

### 1. Non-Violence

We commit to ensuring this software is not used to promote, facilitate, or
advance violence—whether physical, psychological, or institutional—against any
person or group.

**This includes:**

- **Sexual and gender-based violence**: This software shall not be weaponized to
  enable sexual violence, harassment, coercion, exploitation, or gender-based
  discrimination.

- **Violence against marginalized communities**: We explicitly reject the use of
  this software to harm, discriminate against, or exclude people based on sexual
  orientation, gender identity, race, ethnicity, religion, disability,
  neurodiversity, immigration status, or any other characteristic.

- **Trans and LGBTQIA+ safety**: This software must never be used to enforce
  trans-exclusionary ideologies, enable conversion therapy, deny trans and
  non-binary identities, or facilitate harm against LGBTQIA+ people. We reject
  so-called "gender critical" feminism and other movements that weaponize one
  group's rights to erase trans and non-binary people. Political frameworks that
  require the elimination of trans and non-binary people from affirming
  community spaces, that deny gender-affirming healthcare, that reduce them to
  caricatures or threats: this is not liberation. It is collaboration with
  oppression. We will not participate.

- **Systemic discrimination**: This software will not be deployed to reinforce,
  enable, or accelerate systemic discrimination and oppression in any form.

**What this does NOT prohibit:**

- Ideological critique, satire, and passionate opposition to harmful ideologies
- Speech that parodies unjust systems or laws
- Intellectual disagreement and rigorous debate
- Irony and sharp language in service of liberation

Irony is not violence. Speech is not violence. The weaponization of marginalized
people against other marginalized people is violence. We reject movements that
claim to defend marginalized communities by attacking trans and non-binary
people—this is not defense, it is complicity with oppression.

---

### 2. Anti-Exploitation

We believe earning a living through commerce is moral. Extracting wealth through
the degradation, exhaustion, or suffering of others is not.

This software must not be used as an instrument of exacerbated capital
accumulation at the expense of human wellbeing—emotional, mental, or physical.
It may support businesses; it must not support the systematic reduction of human
beings to disposable economic units.

**We resist:**

- The treatment of workers as costs to be minimized rather than people to be
  valued
- Business models predicated on extracting maximum value from human suffering or
  exploitation
- Wages insufficient for dignity, safety, or security
- Working conditions that degrade physical or mental health
- The denial of worker agency, organization, and collective power

---

### 3. Anti-Concentration

This software shall not be deployed with the primary intent to concentrate
excessive wealth or corporate power in fewer hands. Inequality exists; we need
not accelerate its velocity.

We are skeptical of technologies designed to:

- Consolidate wealth into extractive corporate structures
- Eliminate worker autonomy and replace it with algorithmic management
- Centralize power in ways that reduce human agency
- Create technological lock-in that advantages the powerful

---

### 4. Anti-Military

This software shall not be weaponized for military purposes, weapons
development, warfare, or the facilitation of armed conflict.

**This explicitly includes:**

- Direct military applications or defense systems
- Weapons targeting or autonomous weapons systems
- Military surveillance or intelligence gathering
- Defense contractor applications used to facilitate violence or armed
  conflict
- Ballistic calculations, targeting algorithms, or combat support systems
- Armed drone technology or remote warfare systems
- Any use that enables, facilitates, or accelerates military violence or war

We reject the narrative that military necessity justifies technological
advancement. Innovation born from instruments of death is not progress. We
refuse to legitimize warfare through the fiction of "innovation spillover."
There is no noble purpose served by pretending military applications are
acceptable costs of technological development.

The technologies we build shape the possibilities of violence. We choose to
shape them toward peace, not war.

---

### 5. Anti-Racism and Anti-Xenophobia

We reject racism, xenophobia, and all ideologies predicated on the false belief
that humans can be meaningfully divided into discrete biological "races."

**Scientific clarity**: Modern genetics has conclusively established that there
is no such thing as biological human races. The Human Genome Project revealed
that there is more genetic variation within any human population than between
populations. Human genetic variation is clinal—continuous across geography—not
categorical. All humans share 99.35% of their DNA; all humans are members of a
single biological species. All humans carry genetic ancestry from multiple
continents and ethnic backgrounds. There is no "pure" human population.

**What this means**: Racism is not based on biological reality; it is a social
construct used to justify exploitation, exclusion, and violence. Xenophobia—the
fear and hatred of people from other places—is similarly rooted in ignorance
and used to dehumanize, exclude, and harm.

This software shall not be weaponized to:

- Enable, promote, or facilitate racial discrimination or racial violence
- Target, profile, or surveil people based on racial, ethnic, or national origin
  categories
- Spread racist pseudoscience or misinformation about human genetic differences
- Enable xenophobic violence, exclusion, or displacement of migrants, refugees,
  or people from other nations
- Facilitate discrimination based on immigration status, national origin,
  language, or cultural background
- Amplify hate speech, conspiracy theories, or dehumanizing rhetoric targeting
  ethnic, national, or immigrant communities

We recognize that one of racism's greatest harms is its biological falsity. It
is a system of violence built on lies. We refuse to build technology that
legitimizes, perpetuates, or accelerates those lies.

We also recognize that xenophobia—hostility toward people from other places, or
those perceived as "foreign"—is a mechanism through which racism operates
globally. Both systems serve to divide people, concentrate power, and enable
exploitation.

---

### 6. Anti-Surveillance and Anti-Algorithmic Oppression

We commit to resisting the deployment of this software in ways that:

- Enable mass surveillance or the erosion of human privacy and autonomy
- Weaponize artificial intelligence or automated systems against vulnerable
  populations
- Use algorithmic systems to target, profile, or discriminate against
  marginalized communities
- Reduce human beings to quantifiable data points for manipulation or control

Technology is not neutral. Surveillance is not security. Automation is not
progress when it serves oppression.

---

### 7. Environmental and Social Justice

This software must resist:

- Environmental destruction motivated by profit extraction
- The privatization of essential human goods (water, medicine, shelter,
  knowledge)
- Systemic racism, classism, and the structural oppression of marginalized
  communities
- The treatment of vulnerable populations as externalities in technological
  development

We recognize that environmental destruction and social injustice are intimately
connected. Technology that serves one typically serves both.

## What This Means In Practice

### For Users

If you are using this software, we ask that you consider:

- **Whose interests does this serve?** Will this code reduce or amplify human
  suffering?
- **Who is harmed?** Who bears the costs of this implementation? Who benefits?
- **What alternatives exist?** Is there a less exploitative, less violent way
  to achieve the same goal?
- **Am I complicit?** If I deploy this in this context, what am I participating
  in?

We cannot prevent you from violating these principles. We can only ask you to
examine your conscience.

### For Contributors

If you contribute to this project, you are implicitly agreeing to advance these
principles. This means:

- **Thoughtful code review**: Consider not just whether code works, but whether
  it serves our values
- **Welcoming marginalized voices**: Center those most affected by the decisions
  we make
- **Resisting capitalist logic**: Do not optimize purely for growth, profit, or
  market dominance
- **Community accountability**: Build with the understanding that we answer to
  each other, not just to law

Contribution is a moral act, not merely a technical one.

### For Maintainers

We who maintain this software carry particular responsibility:

- **Vigilance**: Monitor for uses that violate these principles
- **Transparency**: If we become aware of harmful deployment, we will speak
- **Community**: Build spaces where ethical accountability is possible
- **Resistance**: If this project is captured by forces antithetical to these
  values, have the courage to step away

## The Limits of This Document

We acknowledge the limitations of words on a screen. **This document cannot
force ethical behavior.** It cannot prevent a corporation with billions of
dollars from using this code to accelerate exploitation. It cannot stop a
government from weaponizing this software against its own people.

Yet we also acknowledge something else: the very fact that this document exists
is itself a statement. It says that we believe another world is possible. It
says that we refuse the fiction of neutrality. It says that we know our choices
matter, and that we choose differently.

What it can do:

- **Declare our values clearly** so that community members know where we stand
- **Appeal to conscience** among those who might otherwise assume technology is
  neutral
- **Create accountability** within the communities that share these values
- **Establish community norms** that shape how we develop and deploy technology
- **Offer solidarity** to those fighting against violence, exploitation, and
  oppression

This is not a legal enforcement mechanism. This is a covenant. It is a refusal.
It is a commitment to build tools in service of liberation, not domination.

## Attribution & Dignity

While licenses typically require attribution, we emphasize this for moral
reasons as well:

**We are people.** We have names, faces, struggles, and stories. We have
labored in good faith on this code. To erase us from the record is a form of
theft—the theft of legacy, of recognition, of dignity.

When you use this code, please:

- Maintain explicit recognition of contributors in source code and
  documentation
- Use our names, not corporate entities
- Acknowledge both the technical and ethical labor involved
- Remember that code is a human creation, not a natural law

Attribution is not about ego. It is about dignity.

## In Closing

This software is offered in hope. Hope that code can serve humanity rather than
dominate it. Hope that those who build technology will consider not just what is
technically possible, but what is ethically right.

We know we cannot force this. We can only ask.

To those fighting for their existence, their dignity, their right to be: **this
software is yours.** Use it toward liberation.

To those who would weaponize this code against the vulnerable: we see you. And
we refuse you—not through law, but through principle.

To everyone else: consider carefully. The technologies you build and deploy are
not neutral. They shape the world. They shape what kinds of futures are
possible. Choose wisely.

The fact that a document like this must exist—that we must spell out opposition
to conversion therapy, that we must explicitly defend trans and non-binary
people, that we must articulate that workers deserve dignity, that we must
clarify basic genetics to refute racist pseudoscience—is itself a failure of our
civilization.

We do not celebrate this document. We grieve its necessity. But we refuse to be
silent in the face of harm.
